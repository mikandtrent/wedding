Frequently Asked Questions

WHAT TIME SHOULD I ARRIVE?

Our ceremony begins at 2:30 PM. Please arrive no earlier than 1:45pm and no later than 2:15pm.

WHAT IS THE DRESS CODE?

Wear something formal! Please keep in mind that this is a winter wedding, so it may be cold. Additionally, stiletto heels are not ideal as guests may have to walk across the lawn and/or red dirt.

CAN I BRING A PLUS-ONE?

Unfortunately we are only able to accommodate the guests specifically listed on your invitation. Please plan for the named guest(s) only.

IS THERE PARKING AT THE VENUE?

Yes! On-site parking is available for all guests. Please follow the signs from the main gate. Remember to use Irishtown Road and avoid Dumbarton Road, as it is unpaved.

IS THERE ACCOMMODATION AT THE VENUE?

Yes! Glamping tents are available to book directly at Buckland Estate. We've also listed some nearby accommodation options in our Stay section above.

WHAT IF I HAVE DIETARY REQUIREMENTS?

Please include all allergies and dietary requirements for your party in the RSVP form so we can pass these on to our caterers.

WHEN SHOULD I RSVP BY?

Please RSVP by May 15 2027 at the latest. This helps us finalise numbers with our venue and caterers.
